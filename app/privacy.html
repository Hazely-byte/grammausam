Privacy Policy

GramMausam • Last updated: September 2026

Summary: GramMausam is built for farmers and rural communities. We respect your privacy: your session is stored exclusively on your device, location data stays on your device, and we never sell, share, or monetize your information.

1. Information We Collect

When you choose to sign in using Google Identity Services, we receive basic authentication credentials provided by Google:

  • Name and Email Address: Used strictly to identify your account and personalize the app interface.
  • Profile Picture: Used solely to display your avatar in the Settings tab.

2. Local Device Storage

GramMausam operates client-side. The following information is stored strictly within your browser's localStorage on your own device:

  • Your active authentication session token and expiration time.
  • Your language preference (English or हिन्दी) associated with your account.
  • Your display theme choice (Auto, Light, or Dark) and your last selected Gram Panchayat.
  • Temporary weather forecasts cached for offline resilience.

3. Geolocation Data

When you tap "Use my location", your device's browser queries your GPS coordinates locally. GramMausam computes the distance to the nearest pilot Gram Panchayat directly on your device. Your geographic coordinates are never transmitted, logged, or saved on any remote server.

4. Third-Party Services & APIs

GramMausam connects to trusted, open-access public weather and mapping services:

  • Google Identity Services: Provides secure single-sign-on without passwords.
  • Open-Meteo: Provides coarse block-level weather forecasts under the open CC-BY 4.0 license.
  • OpenStreetMap & Leaflet: Provides interactive station mapping under the Open Database License (ODbL).

5. Data Retention & Log Out

When you click "Log out" in the Settings tab, your authentication session is instantly erased from your device's storage, and Google Auto-Select is deactivated. You may clear your preferences at any time by clearing your browser cache.

6. Contact Us

For questions or feedback regarding GramMausam or this Privacy Policy, please contact the SIH26074 Development Team.
